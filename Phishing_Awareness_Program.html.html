PHISHING AWARENESS PROGRAM

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals trick people into sharing passwords, bank details or personal information.

How to Identify Suspicious Emails

  • Unknown sender
  • Urgent messages
  • Spelling mistakes
  • Suspicious links
  • Unexpected attachments

Fake Login Pages

  • Wrong website address
  • No HTTPS
  • Poor design
  • Asks for unnecessary information

Fraudulent Websites

  • Very low prices
  • Copied logos
  • No contact information
  • Fake customer reviews

Social Engineering Techniques

  • Email phishing
  • Smishing (SMS)
  • Vishing (Phone calls)
  • Baiting
  • Pretexting

Security Tips

  • Check website URL
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication
  • Never share passwords
  • Use antivirus software
  • Keep software updated

Case Study

An employee received an email claiming to be from the bank asking to verify the account. The employee clicked the link and entered login details. The attackers stole the credentials. Always verify emails before clicking any link.
